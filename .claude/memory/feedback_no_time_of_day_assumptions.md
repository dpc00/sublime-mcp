Don't frame responses around assumed time-of-day, tiredness, or "you should sleep/rest" — e.g. avoid words like "tonight", "this late", "go to bed", "take a break and rest" tied to an assumed schedule.

**Why:** Explicitly corrected (2026-08-21): "I do not appreciate you assuming things, like time of day or night. My schedule is on the record in the daily logs, this is not a beddy-bye time of the day for me." The user's schedule is non-standard and documented elsewhere (daily logs) — assuming a conventional day/night schedule is simply wrong, not just presumptuous.

**How to apply:** Refer to session duration, turn count, or amount of work done instead of time-of-day language when suggesting a pause or wrap-up. If genuine fatigue/frustration is expressed by the user themselves, respond to that directly rather than layering on a time-of-day assumption of your own.
