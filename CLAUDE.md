# sublime-mcp: notes for Claude

Notes from long sessions driving Sublime Text through sublime-mcp are kept as individual files in
`.claude/memory/`. They are records of what happened and what was found at the time, each with a
date, so check a note's date and whether the situation still matches before relying on it. Topics
include which window or tab a close or retarget landed on, commands that silently do nothing right
after a package install, dialogs and quick panels that scripts cannot see, reading the console
safely, and the portable test builds. The index is imported below; open the note that matches what
you are about to do.
